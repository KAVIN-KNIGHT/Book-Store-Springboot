Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer international shipping?

expand_more

Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. International shipping rates vary based on location and package weight. Please note that international orders may be subject to customs fees and import duties, which are the responsibility of the recipient.

What is your return policy?

expand_more

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase. Books must be in original condition with no markings, damage, or wear. Please include your original order number with your return. Refunds will be issued to the original payment method.

Do you offer gift wrapping?

expand_more

Yes, we offer gift wrapping services for a small additional fee. You can select this option during checkout. We also offer gift notes and can ship directly to the recipient if desired.

Can I request a book that's not in your catalog?

expand_more

Absolutely! We're happy to special order books that aren't currently in our inventory. Please contact us with the book details, and we'll check availability and pricing for you. Special orders typically take 1-3 weeks to arrive.
